Words used on this page

  • Recency (days since last purchase): how long ago the buyer last ordered. A small number is good.
  • Frequency (number of orders): how many separate orders the buyer placed. A big number is good.
  • Spend (total money paid): how much the buyer paid in total. A big number is good.
  • Segment: a group of buyers who behave in a similar way.
  • Share: one part of the whole, shown as a percentage.
  • Average: the total split evenly across the group.
